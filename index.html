The app uses microphone access for the push-to-talk radio feature. The microphone is intended to remain muted by default and is enabled only while the user is actively transmitting or when required for radio operation. Audio may be transmitted through the radio service to other connected users in the selected talk group.

Bluetooth and Nearby Devices

The app uses Bluetooth and nearby-device permissions to connect to supported Bluetooth microphones, speakers, and push-to-talk devices. This may include detecting paired devices, connecting to supported devices, and reading push-to-talk button events.

A foreground service may be used to keep the radio connection and supported Bluetooth push-to-talk device active while the app is running in the background.
